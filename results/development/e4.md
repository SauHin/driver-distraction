# Experiment E4: learning rate

Date: 2026-11-17.
Sources: the `_folds.json` files of `e4_deit3_small_lr1e-4`, `e3_deit3_small_noflip`, and `e4_deit3_small_lr9e-4`, and the `metrics.json` of each fold run.
The model is DeiT-III Small with ImageNet weights and the paper set with no flip (decision P17).
The value 0.0003 is the run `e3_deit3_small_noflip`. The other values change only the learning rate.
Accuracy and F1 are the mean validation scores of the 3 folds in the last epoch. F1 is the macro F1.
Each score is the value of the file times 100, with 2 decimals.

## Scores

The training accuracy is the value of epoch 19 of each fold.

| Learning rate | Accuracy | F1 | F1 of each fold | Training accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0001. | 86.82. | 86.80. | 87.72, 87.92, 84.75. | 99.95, 100.00, 99.97. |
| 0.0003. | 89.85. | 89.39. | 87.63, 92.15, 88.39. | 99.97, 100.00, 99.97. |
| 0.0009. | 84.73. | 84.45. | 82.63, 86.73, 83.99. | 99.92, 99.96, 99.95. |

The mean validation loss is 0.8220 at 0.0001, 0.7811 at 0.0003, and 0.9221 at 0.0009.

## Result

- The default value 0.0003 gives the highest mean F1.
- At one third of the default, the mean F1 is 2.59 points lower. The fold changes are +0.09, -4.23, and -3.64.
- At 3 times the default, the mean F1 is 4.94 points lower. The F1 is lower in each fold, by 5.00, 5.42, and 4.40 points.
- All three values fit the training images to a training accuracy of 99.92 or more.
- Thus the learning rate changes the score on new drivers, not the fit to the training images.
- Training is more sensitive to a higher learning rate than to a lower one in these values.
- The winner of E3 keeps the default learning rate.

## Not checked

- Values between the three values, and more than one seed for each value.
- The error tables of each class and each driver at the two new values.
