## Setup of E1

- All E1 models train all layers, with label smoothing 0.1. The CNN models use the learning rate 0.001.
- The other values come from the R1 config (decision P13). B0 trains the attention layers only, with no label smoothing.
- Thus E1 compares the five models. A comparison of E1 with B0 changes more than one variable.

## Speed on the laptop

- `scripts/measure_speed.py` measures one frame at a time. A frame is a 640x480 RGB image, the size of StateFarm.
- Each frame goes through `eval_transform` and the model. The read of a camera frame is not part of the time.
- The GPU is the RTX 3050 6 GB laptop GPU. The CPU is the Ryzen 7 8845HS, with 8 PyTorch threads.
- The laptop ran on its charger. Each value comes from 50 frames after 10 warm-up frames.
- On the GPU, all models give 50.17 to 61.96 FPS, but their sizes differ by a factor of about 20.
- Thus the frame preparation on the CPU probably limits the GPU speed. The AI did not measure this part apart.

## Result

- DeiT-III Small has the highest F1, 88.34. DeiT-III Base is 0.07 points lower.
- This difference is smaller than the difference between the folds of each model.
- DeiT-III Small has about a quarter of the parameters of DeiT-III Base. Its CPU speed is about 3 times higher.
- All five models reach the live target P8, 5 FPS, on the laptop GPU and CPU. DeiT-III Base reaches 5.89 FPS on the CPU.
- The three CNN models have a lower F1 than the two DeiT-III models.
- With the E1 results only, the rules of section 8.5 select DeiT-III Small for `best` and for `live`.
- E2 and E3 can change this. The owner approves both models.
