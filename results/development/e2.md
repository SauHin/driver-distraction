# Experiment E2: start weights

Date: 2026-11-17.
Sources: the `_folds.json` files of `e1_deit3_small`, `e2_deit3_small_random`, `e1_mobilenetv3_large`, and `e2_mobilenetv3_large_random`, and the `history.csv` of each fold run.
The ImageNet value of each model is its E1 run. The random value changes only the start weights.
Accuracy and F1 are the mean validation scores of the 3 folds in the last epoch. F1 is the macro F1.
Each score is the value of the file times 100, with 2 decimals.

## Scores

The training accuracy is the value of epoch 19 of each fold.

| MobileNetV3-Large. | Random. | 41.75. | 40.87. | 34.48, 51.30, 36.81. | 97.89, 97.55, 97.39. |

## Result

- The ImageNet weights help both models on new drivers, in each fold.
- With random weights, the mean F1 of MobileNetV3-Large is 38.34 points lower. The fold changes are -45.33, -30.00, and -39.70.
- With random weights, MobileNetV3-Large learns the training images, to a training accuracy of 97.39 or more.
- But its validation accuracy is 41.75. Thus the gap to new drivers is very large with random weights.
- With random weights, DeiT-III Small does not learn. Its training accuracy stays at 22.83 or less. See `e2_checks.md`.
- Thus the answer to E2 is yes: with 18 development drivers, pretraining is necessary for new drivers.

## Not checked

- The scores of each class and each driver with random weights.
