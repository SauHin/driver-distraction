# Experiment E5: number of training drivers

Date: 2026-11-20.
Sources: the `_folds.json` files of `e5_deit3_small_4drivers`, `e5_deit3_small_8drivers`, and `e3_deit3_small_noflip`, and the `history.csv` of each fold run.
The model is DeiT-III Small with ImageNet weights, the paper set with no flip, and the learning rate 0.0003 (decision P21).
Each fold keeps 4 or 8 of its 12 training drivers. The value 12 is the run `e3_deit3_small_noflip`. The validation drivers stay the same.
Accuracy and F1 are the mean validation scores of the 3 folds in the last epoch. F1 is the macro F1.
Each score is the value of the file times 100, with 2 decimals.

## Scores

The training images are the images of one epoch. The training accuracy is the value of epoch 19.

| Training drivers | Training images | Accuracy | F1 | F1 of each fold | Training accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4. | 3328, 3840, 3456. | 77.63. | 76.57. | 79.73, 77.53, 72.45. | 100.00, 100.00, 100.00. |
| 8. | 6528, 7808, 7424. | 86.11. | 86.03. | 81.13, 88.11, 88.84. | 100.00, 100.00, 99.99. |
| 12. | 10496, 10496, 10496. | 89.85. | 89.39. | 87.63, 92.15, 88.39. | 99.97, 100.00, 99.97. |

The mean validation loss is 1.0774 with 4 drivers, 0.8616 with 8 drivers, and 0.7811 with 12 drivers.

## Result

- More training drivers give a higher score on new drivers.
- From 4 to 8 drivers, the mean F1 rises by 9.46 points. From 8 to 12 drivers, it rises by 3.36 points.
- Thus each added driver helps less, but the score does not reach a limit at 12 drivers.
- With 8 drivers, the F1 of fold 2 is 0.45 points above the value with 12 drivers. The other two folds are lower.
- All values fit the training images to a training accuracy of 99.97 or more. Thus fewer drivers give a larger gap to new drivers.
- This agrees with the fit analysis of B0: the gap to new drivers is the main problem. See `b0_fit.md`.

## Not checked

- Each value has one subset of drivers for each fold. Another subset can give other scores.
- All runs have 20 epochs. Thus a run with fewer drivers has fewer training steps.
