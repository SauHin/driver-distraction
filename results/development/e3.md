# Experiment E3: augmentation

Date: 2026-11-17.
Sources: `e1_deit3_small_folds.json`, `e3_deit3_small_noaug_folds.json`, `e3_deit3_small_noflip_folds.json`, and the `history.csv` of each fold run.
The model is DeiT-III Small with ImageNet weights (decision D16). The paper set is the run `e1_deit3_small`.
Accuracy and F1 are the mean validation scores of the 3 folds in the last epoch. F1 is the macro F1.
Each score is the value of the file times 100, with 2 decimals.

## Scores

The training accuracy is the value of epoch 19 of each fold.

| Augmentation | Accuracy | F1 | F1 of each fold | Training accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| None. | 82.39. | 81.77. | 77.64, 86.13, 81.55. | 100.00, 100.00, 100.00. |
| The paper set. | 88.62. | 88.34. | 87.05, 92.95, 85.03. | 99.96, 99.99, 99.95. |
| The paper set with no flip. | 89.85. | 89.39. | 87.63, 92.15, 88.39. | 99.97, 100.00, 99.97. |

- "None" has no random step. The random crop becomes a center crop (decision P15).
- The mean validation loss is 0.9616 with no augmentation, 0.8197 with the paper set, and 0.7811 with no flip.

## Result

- Augmentation helps. With no augmentation, the mean F1 is 6.57 points lower than with the paper set.
- With no augmentation, the F1 of each fold is lower, by 9.41, 6.82, and 3.49 points.
- The training accuracy is 100.00 with no augmentation. Thus the gap to new drivers is larger.
- The flip does not help. With no flip, the mean F1 is 1.04 points higher than with the paper set.
- The change of each fold is +0.58, -0.80, and +3.35 points. Thus the gain is small and not in each fold.
- The paper set with no flip has the highest mean F1 of E3. Thus it wins E3 (decision P16).
