# Consent form for a recording

Project: driver distraction detection, final project of COMP6826001 Deep Learning, BINUS University.
The owner can translate this form. The meaning must stay the same.

## What the project does

The project trains a computer model that finds a distracted driver in a camera image.
Your recording tests this model. It does not train a public model.

## What we record

- A video of you in the seat of the driver of a parked car. The engine is off.
- You do a list of actions, for example texting, drinking, and talking.
- The video shows your face, your upper body, and your hands.

## How we keep the files

- The files stay on the private computer and the private drive of the project team.
- The file names use a code, not your name.
- The report shows no image of you, unless you agree below.
- The files are deleted after the course ends.

## Your rights

- You can stop at any time, and you need not give a reason.
- You can ask us to delete your files at any time before the course ends.
